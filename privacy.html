Data Stored

If Firebase is configured and you log in, your resume data is saved under your user account in Firestore.

Guest Drafts

Guest resumes are saved in the browser localStorage only and are not uploaded to the cloud.

Contact Messages

Contact form messages may be saved in Firestore if Firebase is configured.

Security

Use the included Firestore security rules and add only trusted domains to Firebase Authentication.
